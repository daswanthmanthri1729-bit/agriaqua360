Cultivation & Agronomy

5 Key Protocol Units

Perform two deep dry plowings to pulverize clods, followed by 10 days of submergence. Execute thorough wet puddling and plank leveling to suppress weed emergence and prevent nutrient leaching.

tips_and_updates Field must be dead-level to guarantee uniform 3cm flood depth.

Utilize 20–25 kg/hectare of certified foundation seed (BPT 5204). Treat pre-soaked seeds with Carbendazim 50% WP (2g/kg seed) or biological Trichoderma viride (5g/kg) to shield against seed-borne foot rot.

Transplant 21–25 day-old seedlings from raised nursery beds. Establish 20 × 15 cm hill spacing with 2 to 3 vigorous culms per hill. Avoid transplanting deeper than 3 cm to foster quick nodal root anchorage.

Administer NPK in a calibrated 120:60:40 kg/ha ratio. Apply 100% P and 50% K as basal dressing; divide Nitrogen into 3 equal splits: Basal, Active Tillering (Day 25), and Panicle Initiation (Day 60).

Deploy Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD) using field water monitoring tubes post-tillering. Allow water table to drop 15 cm below soil surface before re-flooding to conserve up to 25% canal water without yield reduction.
