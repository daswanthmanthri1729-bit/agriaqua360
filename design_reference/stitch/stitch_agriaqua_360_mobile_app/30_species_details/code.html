Technical Overview

Highly domesticated penaeid shrimp species renowned for rapid growth rates, tolerance to wide salinity gradients (2–35 ppt), and exceptional high-density suitability under intensive aeration regimes. Ideal for multi-phase culture operations across coastal and inland brackish aquifers.

Check-Tray Consumption Protocol

Monitor 4 submerged check-trays per pond. If feed is completely cleared within 1.5 – 2.0 hours, increment feeding rate by 5%. If uneaten, reduce next ration by 15% to avoid bottom sediment deterioration.
