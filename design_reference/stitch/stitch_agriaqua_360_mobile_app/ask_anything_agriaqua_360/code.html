Immediate Critical Cause

Pre-dawn Dissolved Oxygen (DO) depletion at the benthic sediment layer. Phytoplankton consumes oxygen overnight without sunlight photosynthesis.

Immediate Recommended Protocol:
1

Switch on all 4 paddle-wheel aerators immediately. Continue aeration until minimum DO reading exceeds 4.5 mg/L on telemetry.

2

Withhold 40% of the first morning feed ration to prevent sudden microbial ammonia spike and bio-oxygen demand surge.

3

Apply granular oxygen enhancer tablets (Sodium Percarbonate @ 5 kg/ha) along pond perimeter if DO remains below 3.0 mg/L.
